Privacy Policy for Saber Cut

Last updated: October 2026

Saber Cut is a simple offline game. This page explains, in plain terms, what the app does and does not do with your information.

We don't collect any data

Saber Cut does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data, usage data, or device identifiers. The app makes no network requests at all — everything it needs (code, images, fonts) is bundled inside the app itself.

No accounts

There is no sign-up, login, or account of any kind. You can start playing the moment you open the app.

No ads, no tracking

Saber Cut contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking software (no SDKs that phone home, no crash reporters, no third-party trackers).

No in-app purchases

The app has no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Your progress stays on your device

Your coins, unlocked heroes, selected hero, best round, and sound setting are saved only on your device. This information never leaves your phone and is never sent to us or anyone else. If you delete the app, this saved progress is deleted with it.

Children's privacy

Because Saber Cut collects no data of any kind from anyone, it does not collect data from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted on this page.
