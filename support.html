Common questions

Is there anything to buy?
No. Saber Cut has no in-app purchases and no ads. Heroes unlock with coins you earn by playing.

My progress didn't carry over to a new device.
Coins, heroes and your best round are saved only on your device and aren't synced between devices.

How do I turn the sound off?
Tap "Sound: on" on the title screen. The game also respects your phone's silent switch.
